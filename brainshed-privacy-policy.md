## What we collect

| Information | Examples | Why we use it |
|---|---|---|
| Account details | Email address, password, user ID | To create your account and sign you in. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see them. |
| Your content | Notes, tags, nudge dates and times | To store, show and sync your notes and send your reminders |
| Preferences and usage within the app | Theme, reminder settings, streaks, note counts | To provide the features you choose and apply usage limits |
| Purchase information | Product bought, date, store transaction identifiers, purchase status | To unlock Premium and restore purchases |
| Technical information | IP address, device type, operating system, app version | Processed by our service providers to run and secure their services |

We do not receive your card or bank details. Payments are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

We do not sell your personal information, show advertising or track you across other apps and websites.

- **Legal obligation:** where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

## Where your data is stored

Your notes are stored in Google Cloud data centres in the **United States**. Our providers may also process information in other countries. If you are in the EU, UK or elsewhere outside the United States, your information is transferred overseas. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or other approved mechanisms in our providers' data processing terms.

Reminders are delivered as notifications on your device. A copy of your notes may also be cached on your device for offline use.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and encrypted at rest by our database provider. Access rules limit each account to its own data. Notes are **not end-to-end encrypted**, so we are technically able to access them. We do so only if needed to provide support you request, protect the service or comply with the law. No system is completely secure.

## How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete individual notes at any time.

When you delete your account, we delete your account, notes, nudges and preferences within 30 days. Residual copies may remain for a limited time in our providers' backups and logs. Purchase records may be kept by the app stores and RevenueCat for accounting, fraud prevention and restoring purchases. Contact us to ask for your RevenueCat record to be removed.

## Deleting your account

- **In the app:** Preferences > Account > Delete account
- **Without the app:** email glennp3001@googlemail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my Brainshed account". We may need to verify that the account is yours.

Deleting your account does not refund a purchase.

## Exporting your data

Your notes are yours. You can export them from the app as Markdown text. For a copy of other personal information we hold, contact us.

Brainshed is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
